“Songs for Fallen Drones” is strong enough to become a proper concept album rather than just a pile of comedy songs. I’d make it tell the entire tiny-whoop lifecycle: confidence → Acro → crash → search → diagnosis → repair → financial ruin → delusions of sponsorship → recovery → immediate crash again.
My 12-track version would be:
1. I Suck at Tiny Whoop — opening statement
   Piano-led satirical singer-songwriter. Establishes the protagonist: enthusiastic, hopeless, and completely aware of it.
2. Acro Mode Is Fucking Hard — the training montage
   Upbeat acoustic comedy-folk. Doorways, overcorrections, ceiling, floor, Crashflip. This should be one of the catchy singles.
3. Telemetry Lost — first major tragedy
   Ridiculous 80s power ballad. The robotic “TELEMETRY LOST” interruptions are too good not to keep.
4. National Tiny Whoop Emergency — the crash
   Full disaster-movie orchestra, sirens, news reports, government response. One of the centerpiece tracks.
5. Under the Sofa Again — recovery
   Gentle cheerful folk after the enormous drama. Crawling around while the stream watches an empty chair.
6. Operation Tiny Screw — the repair begins
   Military/national-emergency epic over an M1.4 screw. Definitely keep this one. It’s probably one of the funniest concepts we hit.
7. Motor Three Is Hot — things get worse
   Hazmat/disaster sequel. Starts as diagnosis, escalates into a screw emergency, ends with motor four becoming hot. Perfect continuation.
8. FPV Is Expensive When You Suck — financial consequences
   Country/comedy singalong. Props, frames, motors, batteries, free shipping over €50. This is the relatable FPV anthem.
9. This Crash Brought to You By… — commercial break
   Fake corporate advertising song. Turns your inability to stay airborne into sponsor inventory.
10. The Trial of the Pilot — accountability
    Giant courtroom rock opera. Expert witness concludes the only defective component is the pilot. This should be late in the album when all the evidence has accumulated.
11. IMPACT — artistic breakdown
    Completely absurd perfume-ad art-pop interlude: “A hint of hot motor, a trace of burnt dust…” It gives the album a weird left turn and stops all the comedy from sounding the same.
12. One More Pack — the finale — this is the big missing song
    Starts quietly and almost emotionally sincere. You’ve repaired everything. You’ve spent the money. You’ve learned your lesson. It’s late. You really should stop. Then: “One more battery…” The whole cast gradually joins in. Huge triumphant finale. You arm the drone. BONK. Album ends.
