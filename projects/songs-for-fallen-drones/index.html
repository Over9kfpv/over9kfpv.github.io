    About the Fund

    Every 30 seconds

    a tiny whoop somewhere hits a lamp. Most are never spoken of again. Some are glued back together and sent out for one more pack.

    The Fallen Drones Relief Fund was set up to make sure no whoop crashes alone. All proceeds go to replacement Prop Number Threes.
